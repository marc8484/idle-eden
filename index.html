New Player Guide

Welcome to Idle Eden

Idle Eden is a browser-based idle RPG inspired by the realms, classes, and frontier warfare of Dark Age of Camelot. Create a roster of heroes, send them into combat, improve their gear, and fight for your realm. Idle Eden is an independent game simulation and is not connected to the DAoC live servers.

Getting Started

  • 1. Create a character. Open the Character tab, choose a name, realm, and class, then create your hero.
  • 2. Start fighting. Return to Combat. Your character battles automatically; use your class ability when it is ready.
  • 3. Grow stronger. Earn XP, Realm Points, and Gold. Find equipment, equip upgrades, and travel through frontier zones as you progress.
  • 4. Craft and trade. Gather materials in Crafting, brew healing potions, and turn eligible bag items into Gems. Spend Gems on the rotating Rare and Epic offers in the Gem Merchant tab.
  • 5. Explore the war. Visit World, RvR, and War Room to follow realm battles, arena matches, keeps, and battlefield activity.

What You Can Work Toward

  • Progress through 446 frontier zones. Reach level 1,000, then earn Mastery ranks to unlock the remaining zones.
  • Build a team of eight level-capped characters, gear every slot in Epic items, and challenge the five endgame elite bosses. Defeating them all earns the game's ultimate achievement.
  • Your progress saves locally automatically. Google sign-in in the Character tab provides optional cloud backups.

Idle Eden is actively evolving, and some realm-war activity is simulated within the game. Welcome to the frontier!

A local frontier simulation brings realm armies to life as you hunt, and every five PvE kills brings the keeps closer to battle.

Added

  • A local frontier battlefield map with realm camps, marching armies, keep ownership, and a live battle report.
  • Every five PvE kills triggers a keep battle. Captures by your realm reward 75 RP and 25 Gold, adjusted by relic bonuses.
  • First Blood: defeat 1 enemy; Veteran: defeat 1,000.
  • Champion Hunter: defeat 25 Frontier raid bosses; Relic Master: find 50 Artifacts.
